# Company FAQ — Everline Home Services

## About the company
- Everline Home Services has served the Austin metro since 2009.
- Licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Texas (TACLA license for HVAC,
  RMP-licensed master plumber on staff, TDLR-licensed electricians).
- All technicians are background-checked and drug-tested. Technicians arrive in a
  marked van and wear a photo ID badge.

## Guarantees and warranties
- 1-year labor warranty on all repairs.
- Manufacturer parts warranties passed through to the customer (typically 5–10 years
  on new HVAC equipment when registered).
- 100% satisfaction guarantee: if the problem recurs within 30 days of a repair,
  we return at no charge.

## Common questions
- **Do technicians wear shoe covers?** Yes, always inside the home.
- **Will I get a heads-up before arrival?** Yes — a text/call when the technician is
  on the way, with the technician's name.
- **Can I get a receipt/invoice by email?** Yes, invoices are emailed automatically.
- **Do you install customer-supplied parts or equipment?** No — we cannot warranty
  parts we didn't supply, so we install only equipment we provide.
- **Are estimates really free?** Yes for full replacements and new installations.
  Diagnostic visits for repairs have an $89 fee, waived if you proceed with the repair.
- **How fast can someone come for an emergency?** For critical emergencies our target
  arrival window is 60–90 minutes, 24/7.
- **Do you offer rental property / landlord services?** Yes, we work with landlords and
  property managers; the tenant can be on site while billing goes to the owner.
