# Pricing Policy — Everline Home Services

## Golden rule for quoting on the phone
We NEVER quote a firm price for repair work over the phone. Nearly every job is
diagnostic-dependent — the honest answer is "it depends on what the technician
finds." What we CAN quote confidently:

## Service call / diagnostic fees (these are fixed and can be quoted)
- Standard diagnostic visit (HVAC, plumbing, or electrical): $89, waived if the customer proceeds with the repair.
- After-hours / weekend emergency dispatch fee: $149, also credited toward the repair if they proceed.
- Free estimates: full system replacements (new AC, new furnace, new water heater, panel upgrade) get a free in-home estimate — no diagnostic fee.

## Typical price RANGES (may be shared as rough ranges only, always with the caveat "the exact price depends on diagnosis")
- AC repair: most repairs fall between $150 and $650.
- Capacitor replacement: $150–$280. Refrigerant recharge: $200–$500 depending on type and amount.
- Furnace repair: most repairs fall between $150 and $600.
- Water heater replacement: $1,400–$3,200 installed (tank); tankless $2,800–$5,500.
- Drain clearing: $120–$350 for standard drains; main sewer line $250–$600.
- Burst pipe repair: $250–$1,200 depending on access and location.
- Toilet replacement: $350–$700 installed.
- Panel upgrade (200 amp): $1,800–$3,500.
- EV charger installation: $450–$1,500 depending on panel capacity and wire run.
- HVAC seasonal tune-up: $99 flat (this one IS a firm price).

## Payment and financing
- We accept all major credit cards, checks, and cash.
- Financing available on jobs over $1,000 through third-party financing, subject to approval.
- Seniors (65+) and veterans get 10% off repairs, up to $200 maximum discount.

## Membership plan
- "Everline Comfort Club": $19/month. Includes two seasonal HVAC tune-ups per year,
  15% off all repairs, no after-hours dispatch fee, and priority scheduling.
