# Services Offered — Everline Home Services

## HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning)
- AC repair: no cooling, weak airflow, unit not turning on, frozen coils, refrigerant leaks, strange noises.
- Heating repair: furnace not igniting, no heat, heat pump issues, thermostat problems, pilot light out.
- AC and furnace installation and replacement (free in-home estimate required, scheduled as a "comfort consultation").
- Seasonal maintenance and tune-ups: recommended twice a year (spring for AC, fall for heating).
- Duct cleaning and duct repair.
- Thermostat installation, including smart thermostats (Nest, Ecobee, Honeywell).

## Plumbing
- Emergency plumbing: burst pipes, major leaks, no water, sewage backup, gas water heater leaks.
- Drain cleaning and unclogging: sinks, tubs, showers, main sewer line.
- Water heater repair and replacement (tank and tankless).
- Toilet repair and replacement.
- Faucet, garbage disposal, and fixture installation.
- Leak detection (slab leaks, hidden wall leaks).
- Sump pump service.

## Electrical
- Emergency electrical: total power loss (when the utility is not the cause), burning smell from outlets or panel, sparking outlets, exposed wiring.
- Panel upgrades and breaker replacement.
- Outlet, switch, and lighting installation.
- Ceiling fan installation.
- EV charger installation (Level 2 home chargers).
- Whole-home surge protection.
- Electrical safety inspections.

## What we do NOT do
- Appliance repair (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens) — refer the caller to an appliance repair specialist.
- New-construction full-home wiring or plumbing (we do remodels and additions, not ground-up new builds).
- Septic tank pumping — we service the lines to the tank, but pumping requires a septic company.
- Window AC units — we service central air and mini-splits only.
