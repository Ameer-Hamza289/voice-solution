# Emergency Triage Protocol — Everline Home Services

## Severity levels
- **CRITICAL (dispatch immediately, any hour)**: active water flooding or burst pipe,
  sewage backing up into the home, gas smell near a water heater or furnace,
  sparking outlet or burning electrical smell, total power loss affecting medical equipment,
  no heat when outdoor temperature is below 40°F, no AC when outdoor temperature is above 95°F
  and someone in the home is elderly, an infant, or medically vulnerable.
- **URGENT (same-day or next-morning slot)**: no AC in summer or no heat in winter without
  vulnerability factors, water heater leaking slowly, one non-functional bathroom in a
  multi-bath home, refrigerator circuit tripping repeatedly.
- **ROUTINE (next available standard slot)**: maintenance, tune-ups, installations,
  estimates, slow drains, dripping faucets, ceiling fan install, noisy but working equipment.

## Safety instructions the agent MUST give before anything else
- **Gas smell**: "Please leave the house immediately, don't flip any switches or light anything,
  and call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line from outside. Once you're safe, we can get
  a technician out." Do not book anything until the caller confirms they are safely outside.
- **Burst pipe / major leak**: tell the caller to shut off the main water valve — usually near
  the water meter at the street, in the garage, or where the main line enters the house.
- **Sparking outlet / burning smell**: tell the caller to switch off that circuit at the breaker
  panel and unplug devices on the circuit. If there are flames or smoke, call 911 first.
- **Sewage backup**: avoid contact with the water, keep kids and pets away.
- **No cooling with vulnerable person in home during extreme heat**: suggest they move the
  vulnerable person to the coolest room or a neighbor's until the technician arrives.

## Dispatch rules
- CRITICAL: create a dispatch ticket immediately. After-hours emergency fee ($149) applies
  and must be disclosed before confirming. Typical arrival window: 60–90 minutes.
- URGENT: offer the earliest same-day slot; if none, first slot next morning.
- ROUTINE: offer next available standard slots, let the caller choose.
- Always collect: full name, callback phone number, complete street address with ZIP code,
  and a short issue description before confirming any dispatch or booking.
- Verify the address ZIP code is inside our service area BEFORE promising anyone.
